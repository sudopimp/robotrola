
# Safety and Legal Disclaimer

Robotrola Core is an experimental robotics R&D repository. It is not a medical device, consumer robot, certified collaborative robot, certified appliance, or certified intimate product. Do not operate near people until all mechanical, electrical, thermal, firmware, software, and privacy risks are professionally reviewed and validated.

The included CAD/STL files are reference geometry for packaging, fit checks, research fixtures, and non-load-bearing prototype shells. They are not certified structural parts. Replace them with professionally engineered parts before load-bearing use.

All adult companionship functionality must be restricted to consenting adults in jurisdictions where such use is legal. The repository intentionally keeps high-risk or explicit modules feature-flagged, non-explicit, and disabled by default.
