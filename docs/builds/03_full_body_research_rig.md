# Build 03 — Full-body research rig

Goal: integrate full visual body shell and lower-body sensors after upper-body validation.

## Strict constraints

- safety cage for locomotion experiments
- certified battery or bench tether only
- fall arrest or support rig
- dummy-only interaction tests
- no unsupported standing near people

## Required before this phase

- measured masses and inertias
- validated URDF/digital twin
- actuator thermal data
- full power review
- risk register updated
